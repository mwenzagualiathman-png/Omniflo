WEBOmniFlo Guide

I Bought a Domain. Why Doesn't My Website Work?

Domain, hosting, DNS and the simple chain that makes a website reachable.

Buying a domain gives you an address, not the whole website. Your website files need hosting, and DNS needs to point that address to the correct hosting service. When those pieces disagree, visitors can see broken pages or nothing at all.

OmniFlo lesson: think of the domain as the address, hosting as the building, and DNS as the directions connecting the two.
AIOmniFlo Guide

What AI Can Actually Do for a Small Business

Where AI can save time, improve output and help a small team work more effectively.

AI can help with research, drafting, summarising, idea generation, customer-response preparation, data organisation and repetitive content workflows. The useful question is not “What can AI do?” but “Which repetitive or information-heavy task should I improve first?”
WEBOmniFlo Guide

Why Is My Website Not Loading?

A beginner's checklist for diagnosing a website that suddenly stops working.

Check the domain, DNS, hosting status, deployment status, SSL certificate and the exact error message. Change one thing at a time. If a site works on a Netlify preview URL but not the custom domain, the problem is often in domain or DNS configuration rather than the website files.
BUSINESSOmniFlo Guide

From Website to Online Business: What Comes Next?

A website becomes valuable when it gives visitors a useful reason to stay, return or take action.

A strong online business can connect useful content, products, services, email capture, recommendations and customer journeys. The website is the hub; each useful piece should have a clear purpose and a measurable next step.
