## How AI Is Changing Cybersecurity: Benefits, Risks and Protection

Introduction

Artificial intelligence is changing how businesses protect their information and handle digital threats.

Today, AI can help detect suspicious activities, identify security weaknesses and respond to potential cyberattacks.

But there is another side: cybercriminals can also use AI to create more convincing scams and sophisticated attacks.

So, is AI making the internet safer? Let's explore.

#### How AI Improves Cybersecurity

AI helps security teams analyse large amounts of data and identify unusual activities much faster.

It can help organisations:

* Detect suspicious login attempts.
* Identify software vulnerabilities.
* Recognise potential cyberattacks.
* Monitor networks for unusual behaviour.
* Support faster security investigations.

For example, OpenAI announced new cybersecurity initiatives in November 2026, demonstrating how AI agents can help identify software vulnerabilities and support security teams.

#### The Risks of AI in Cybersecurity

AI is not only useful to security professionals. Cybercriminals can also misuse it.

Some emerging risks include:

* More convincing phishing emails.
* Fake messages and impersonation scams.
* Automated cyberattack operations.
* Attempts to exploit weaknesses in digital systems.

This means that as AI becomes more powerful, protecting digital information becomes increasingly important.

#### What Businesses Should Do

Small businesses and large organisations both need to take cybersecurity seriously.

Here are five practical steps:

1. Use strong, unique passwords.
2. Enable two-factor authentication.
3. Keep software and devices updated.
4. Back up important business information.
5. Be careful when giving AI tools access to sensitive data.

AI can strengthen security, but basic digital protection remains essential.

#### What Does the Future Look Like?

The future of cybersecurity will involve both artificial intelligence and human expertise.

AI can help detect threats and recommend solutions, while people remain responsible for important decisions and security oversight.

Businesses that understand both the benefits and risks of AI will be better prepared for the changing digital environment.

#### Conclusion

Artificial intelligence is transforming cybersecurity. It offers new ways to protect information, but it also introduces new threats.

The solution is not to avoid AI. It is to understand it and use it responsibly.
