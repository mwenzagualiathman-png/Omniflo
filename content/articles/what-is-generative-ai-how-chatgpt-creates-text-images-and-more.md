What Is Generative AI? How ChatGPT Creates Text, Images and More

Imagine typing a sentence into a computer and receiving a complete article, a realistic image, a voice recording or even a piece of computer code.

That is the power of Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI).

Tools such as ChatGPT have made this technology accessible to millions of people, changing how individuals learn, create content, communicate and work.

But what exactly is generative AI? How does it create something new, and why is it becoming important for businesses and everyday users?

Let's explore.

What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that creates new content, such as text, images, audio, video and computer code, by learning patterns from existing data.

Unlike AI systems designed mainly to classify information or make predictions, generative AI focuses on producing new outputs in response to instructions or prompts.

For example:

- ChatGPT can generate written answers and articles.
- Image-generation tools can create pictures from descriptions.
- AI voice tools can turn written scripts into speech.
- Video-generation tools can create video clips from prompts.
- AI coding assistants can generate and explain computer code.

The important distinction is that generative AI does not simply retrieve a finished answer from a database every time. It uses a trained model to generate an output based on patterns it has learned.

How Does Generative AI Work?

Generative AI works through machine-learning models trained on data. These models learn patterns and relationships that help them produce new content.

Although different technologies are used for text, images, audio and video, the general process can be understood in four stages.

1. Learning From Data

The model is trained using examples relevant to the type of content it is designed to generate.

A language model, for example, learns from text. An image-generation model learns patterns from images and their associated information.

The training process helps the model identify relationships, structures and patterns within the data.

2. Building a Model

During training, the system adjusts its internal parameters to improve its ability to perform a task.

The resulting trained model can use what it has learned to respond to new instructions.

Some models are also adapted or fine-tuned for particular purposes.

3. Receiving a Prompt

A prompt is the instruction or information a user provides to guide the AI.

For example:

"Write a professional product description for a pair of black leather shoes."

The model processes the prompt and uses its learned patterns and available context to generate a response.

4. Generating the Output

The model produces content based on the task and the information provided.

The result might be a paragraph, image, voice recording, video or computer code.

The quality of the result depends on several factors, including the model, prompt, available context and task.

How Does ChatGPT Generate Text?

ChatGPT is an example of a generative AI application powered by large language models.

A large language model learns patterns in language during training. When you ask a question, it processes the prompt and generates a response by predicting successive tokens.

A token can represent a whole word, part of a word or punctuation.

For example, if you ask ChatGPT to explain how solar energy works, the model uses the context of your request to generate a relevant explanation one token at a time.

This process allows it to produce answers, summaries, stories, explanations and other forms of text.

However, generating convincing language does not guarantee that every statement is correct. Important information should still be verified.

What Can Generative AI Create?

Generative AI is not limited to writing. It can produce several types of digital content.

Text

AI writing tools can draft emails, explain concepts, summarise documents, create product descriptions and assist with research.

Images

Image-generation models can create illustrations, product concepts, marketing visuals and artistic designs from written prompts.

Audio and Speech

Generative AI can create synthetic speech, assist with voiceovers and generate or transform audio.

Video

Some AI systems can generate video clips from text or image instructions and help with video-production workflows.

Computer Code

AI coding assistants can generate code, explain programming concepts, identify possible errors and help developers work through technical problems.

These capabilities make generative AI useful across education, marketing, software development, entertainment and business.

Generative AI vs Traditional AI: What's the Difference?

Traditional AI applications are often designed to recognise, classify, recommend or predict.

Generative AI is designed to create content.

Consider an online business:

- A traditional AI system might predict which products a customer is likely to purchase.
- A generative AI system might create a personalised product description or draft a marketing message.

Both approaches can be valuable. The right choice depends on the problem being solved.

Generative AI is also not separate from machine learning. It is a category of AI that commonly uses machine-learning techniques, particularly deep-learning models.

How Can Small Businesses Use Generative AI?

Generative AI offers practical opportunities for small businesses, including businesses in Kenya.

Here are some examples.

1. Marketing content: Generate first drafts of social media captions, promotional messages and advertising ideas.

2. Product descriptions: Create clear descriptions for online shops, clothing sellers and other businesses.

3. Customer communication: Prepare responses to common customer questions and organise frequently asked questions.

4. Business documents: Draft proposals, reports, business plans and professional emails.

5. Visual content: Develop concepts for posters, social media graphics and product imagery.

6. Learning and research: Use AI assistants to explain unfamiliar subjects and organise information for further investigation.

For example, a small clothing seller in Mombasa could use generative AI to prepare product descriptions, create promotional image concepts and draft WhatsApp marketing messages.

The business owner should still check prices, product details, delivery information and all other important claims before publishing.

What Are the Benefits of Generative AI?

Generative AI can offer several advantages:

- Speed: It can produce first drafts and creative ideas quickly.
- Accessibility: People can use natural-language instructions instead of learning complex software first.
- Creativity support: It can help users explore different concepts and approaches.
- Productivity: It can assist with repetitive writing, summarising and content-production tasks.
- Learning support: It can explain complicated subjects in simpler language.

These benefits do not mean every task should be handed over to AI. Human judgement remains important.

What Are the Risks and Limitations?

Generative AI is powerful, but it has limitations that users should understand.

Incorrect information: AI can produce convincing answers that contain factual errors.

Privacy: Entering sensitive personal or business information into an AI tool may create privacy risks. Always understand the tool's data policies.

Copyright and ownership: Questions about training data, generated content and intellectual property can be complex. Users should understand the rules that apply to their situation.

Bias: Models can reproduce unfair patterns present in their training data.

Human oversight: Generated documents, images, code and business decisions should be reviewed before use.

The safest approach is to treat generative AI as an assistant, not an unquestionable authority.

What Is the Future of Generative AI?

Generative AI is developing beyond simple question-and-answer tools.

It is increasingly being integrated into software, business processes, education, creative production and digital services.

The technology is also moving towards systems that can work with multiple forms of information, including text, images, audio and video.

For individuals and businesses, the opportunity is not simply to use the newest AI tool. It is to understand how the technology can solve real problems.

Frequently Asked Questions About Generative AI

Is ChatGPT generative AI?

Yes. ChatGPT is a generative AI application that uses large language models to generate responses to user prompts.

Can generative AI create images?

Yes. Image-generation models can produce images from text prompts and, depending on the tool, other forms of input.

Is generative AI the same as machine learning?

No. Generative AI is a category of AI that commonly uses machine-learning techniques to create new content. Machine learning is a broader field.

Can generative AI replace human workers?

Generative AI can automate or assist with certain tasks, but its effects vary across jobs and industries. Human expertise, judgement, accountability and supervision remain important.

Can small businesses use generative AI?

Yes. Small businesses can use it to assist with marketing, product descriptions, customer communication, documents, research and creative work.

Final Thoughts: Generative AI Is a Tool for Creating Value

Generative AI is changing how people create and interact with digital content.

It can generate text, images, audio, video and code, making creative and technical capabilities more accessible.

But the real value of this technology is not simply that it can produce content. Its value comes from how people use it to solve problems, improve their work and create useful solutions.
