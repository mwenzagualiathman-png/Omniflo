Resto Cashier

From paper records to a practical digital system.

Resto Cashier was inspired by a simple problem found in many restaurants: important business records are still written in books.

Finding an old transaction can take time. Books get damaged, lost or stored away. Resto Cashier is being built to make restaurant operations easier to record, manage and review.

✓ Manage restaurant orders
✓ Record payments
✓ Organize menu items
✓ Maintain transaction records
✓ Support restaurant management
